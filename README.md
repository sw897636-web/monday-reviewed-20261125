A make.fun bounty entry: the funniest one-line review of my own Monday, under 200 characters.

## The entry

> Monday, 7:03 a.m.: the lift died on floor 3, my coffee spilled on my clean shirt, and my 9 a.m. meeting moved to 8 — I rated Monday one star and it replied, 'Thanks for your feedback.'

184 characters, one line. Deadpan, concrete (7:03, floor 3, a 9 a.m. meeting moved to 8), and the twist lands in the last clause: Monday answers back like a customer-service bot.

## How it runs

`main.ts` writes the final line to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
node main.ts
```

It also records the drafting rounds — five candidate versions, from a generic complaint to the final line — so the evolution from template to twist is visible in the code.
